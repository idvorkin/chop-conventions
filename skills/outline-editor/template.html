The outline

Drag the ⠿ handle, or use ▲ ▼ to move a row (a heading takes the rows nested under it along). ◀ ▶ change how deep a row sits; in the contents tree, dragging sideways does the same. Dropping on another page moves it there, and pages with a fixed status (like Cut) set it. Tap a card's title for its notes, a status picker and a "move to" picker.
