# Getting Started With Houseplants

A beginner's guide written a few years ago.

## Why keep plants

Plants make a room calmer and teach patience.

## Picking a plant

### Light

Match the plant to the window, not the other way round.

### Size

Start small; repotting is half the fun.

## Watering

Water when the top inch of soil is dry.
